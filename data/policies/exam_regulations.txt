EXAMINATION REGULATIONS

Students must carry their college ID card to all examinations. Entry is not
permitted after 15 minutes from the start of the exam.

Makeup exams are granted only for medical emergencies (with hospital
certificate) or bereavement in immediate family, and must be requested
in writing to the Controller of Examinations within 3 days of the missed exam.

Malpractice during exams results in automatic cancellation of that paper
and a formal disciplinary hearing.
