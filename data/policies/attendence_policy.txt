ATTENDANCE POLICY - Vasavi College of Engineering

Minimum attendance required to be eligible for semester-end examinations is 75%.
Students with attendance between 65% and 74% may apply for condonation through
the Head of Department, subject to valid medical or personal grounds, with
supporting documents submitted within 7 days of the last working day of the semester.

Students with attendance below 65% are not eligible for condonation and must
repeat the course in the next academic cycle.

Attendance is calculated per course, not as an overall average across all courses.
